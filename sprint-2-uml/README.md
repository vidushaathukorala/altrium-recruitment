# Sprint 2 – UML Diagrams

Use case, activity and sequence diagrams for the five Sprint 2 backlog items of the **Recruitment & Hiring Tracker** (Altrium (Private) Limited).  
Prepared by **Vidusha Athukoralage** (Business Analyst, Sprint 2). Live system: https://altrium-recruitment.vercel.app/

**Sprint 2 goal:** complete the post-shortlisting workflow. This covers scheduling interviews around interviewer availability, capturing structured evaluations, comparing candidates, finalising hiring decisions with automatic emails, and helping candidates through a career chatbot.

A Word version with all 15 diagrams is in this folder: `Sprint_2_UML_Diagrams.docx`.
